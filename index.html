How to use

  1. Clean and completely dry your nose.
  2. Peel the protective backing from the strip.
  3. Position ZAELLFLOW across the bridge of your nose, above the nostril flare.
  4. Press both sides firmly onto the skin.
  5. Breathe naturally through your nose.

To remove: slowly peel the strip away from the skin. If necessary, gently soften the adhesive with warm water first.

Safety information

For external use only. Do not apply to broken, irritated, inflamed or damaged skin. Do not use if you are sensitive or allergic to adhesives or any component of the product. Discontinue use if irritation or discomfort occurs. Do not reuse. Keep out of reach of children.

ZAELLFLOW is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. If you experience persistent breathing difficulties, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
